# Contractors

**Partners → Contractors** holds your **upstream** parties — the organizations that give
you transport work.

From their side of the relationship, you are their [subcontractor](./subcontractors).
One relationship, each side seeing half of it.

## The list

**Search contractors** finds them by name, and **Filter by status** narrows:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | Working together |
| Pending | Relationship not yet confirmed |
| Suspended | On hold |
| Inactive | Not active |

The table shows contractor, contact and status. Click through for details.

## Contractors and waybills

Contractor is one of the waybill filters. On the [waybill list](../waybills/list), filter
by **Contractor** to see only goods from one upstream party.

## Contractors vs sender accounts

Both are "people who send you goods", but at different levels:

| | **Contractors** | **Sender Accounts** |
|---|---|---|
| Who | An **organization** outsourcing transport to you | The specific **account** that ships |
| Used for | The business relationship and settlement | Hanging waybills, billing and wallets off |
| Created | When the other party registers and the relationship is formed | By you — see [Sender accounts](./sender-accounts) |
