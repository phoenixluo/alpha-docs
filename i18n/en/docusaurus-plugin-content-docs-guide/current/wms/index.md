# WMS Warehouse Management

**Being written.**

WMS covers the stretch where goods sit in a warehouse: receiving, put-away, stock,
picking and dispatch. It shares the same organization and account model as TMS — within
one organization, warehouse work and transport work see the same goods.

## How it relates to TMS

The two products meet at **dispatch**: a delivery unit in TMS maps to a specific
warehouse in WMS, and when goods leave the warehouse the waybill status in TMS moves on.
How to wire that up is covered in the [Developer Guide](/tms/).
