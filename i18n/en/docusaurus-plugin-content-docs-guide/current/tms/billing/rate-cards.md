# Rate cards

**Billing → Rate Cards** defines what your services charge. Every amount on every
consignment starts here.

## The list

**Search rate cards** finds them by name, and there's a **Filter by service** beside it.

## Creating one

Click **New Rate Card** and set a name, the service it belongs to, and the pricing rules.

Rate cards attach to **services** — which card a service uses is configured on the
service; see the services page in the settings chapter.

## Changing prices carefully

Change a rate card and billing generated **afterwards** uses the new price.
**Existing billing is not recalculated.**

So the right order for a price change is:

1. settle the billing that should go out at the old price;
2. change the rate card;
3. later goods price at the new rate.

## Deleting

Rate cards can be deleted. Check no service still uses one first, or that service can no
longer price its goods.

## Permissions

Changing rate cards needs the separate **Rate Cards** permission. Someone with billing
access can read billing and raise invoices without being able to touch pricing.
