# Drivers

**Partners → Drivers** manages your own drivers: accounts, vehicles and status.

The search box at the top matches **name, email, phone or licence**.

## Two ways to add someone

The two buttons in the top right do different jobs:

| Button | Use it when |
|---|---|
| **Invite Driver** | The driver will confirm deliveries through LINE — see [Delivery confirmation](../driver/pod). Generates a registration link to send them |
| **Add Driver** | You're creating the record yourself. **Name and phone are required** |

**Invite Driver** generates the link and copies it automatically — paste it straight into
LINE. Links expire after about two days; generate a new one if it lapses.

:::note
If you're told the organization has no API credentials, generate them in the
organization settings first, then come back and invite.
:::

## The vehicle decides what they can be given

A driver's record carries their vehicle and vehicle type. That isn't decoration — when
[planning deliveries](../delivery/planning), the driver dropdown **only lists drivers
whose vehicle type matches the trip**.

If a driver is on shift but can't be assigned, check their vehicle type first.

## Active and inactive

Drivers can be switched between **Active** and **Inactive** at any time. Inactive drivers
aren't offered for assignment.

Deactivate rather than delete when someone leaves — past deliveries and tracking still
reference them.
